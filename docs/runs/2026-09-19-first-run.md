## Reading the result against intuition (the step 10 test)

The roadmap says: where the computed number disagrees badly with gut, the tree is probably missing a dependency or a node; fix the tree, not the number. Three results fail that test.

**The Belt does not exist by 2095 (Tier 3 at 0.2 percent), and the headline (A2) is 0.1 percent.** Every individual node in the chain is between 0.3 and 0.9 by 2095, so the near-zero comes from multiplying a long conjunction of requirements as if they were independent, when most of them share causes (cheap launch, sustained funding, a working off-Earth economy). The outside forecaster (Astra, 2026-09-19) made the same point about its own seven marginals. The compute script's independence assumption is a known simplification (docs/node-schema.md), and this is the first time it has bitten.

**Tier 1 (an outpost, hundreds of people) is 9 percent by 2071.** That is lower than any informed reader would put it. The cause is two regime nodes used as hard gates:

- `R-crewed-lunar-program-outlives-two-changes-of-president` (0.60, flat) is a Tier 1 requirement and a hard dependency of lunar material at scale. Its criterion resolves no by about 2037 if one administration cuts the program, and a no is permanent, so a single cut in the 2030s forecloses an outpost forever, by anyone, including China or a private operator. The node is a good signal and a bad gate.
- `R-space-funding-holds-through-a-recession` (0.55, flat) is a hard dependency of a hundred people working off Earth for pay. It resolves at the first recession, and a no is permanent, so one bad budget year in the 2020s forecloses a hundred people off Earth in 2136. Same fault.

**Tier 2 (a settlement) is 1.8 percent by 2095, and it gates everything above it.** The choke is `B-human-child-born-off-earth` at 0.147 by 2095, required for Tier 2 and therefore for Tier 3, A2 and A3. The definition of Tier 2 does say a child born and raised off Earth, so the requirement is faithful; the question is whether the headline (working off Earth on rotation) should wait on it. A rotation workforce of tens of thousands could exist at a site where nobody has yet raised a child.

## Proposed fixes, for John to rule on (each with confidence, whether it is standard practice, and the strongest alternative, per the ground rule)

1. **Reword the two regime gates so a no is not permanent.** Change the criteria from a single test at a fixed event to a durable form: the lunar program (any nation's or company's crewed lunar program, not only the US one) keeps flying across two changes of government; and space spending recovers to its pre-recession level within three years of any recession rather than never dipping. Confidence: high that the current criteria are the fault; moderate on the exact rewording. Standard practice: yes, a prerequisite in a dependency graph should be something that can still come true later, not a one-shot test. Alternative: keep the criteria as written and remove the nodes from the tier requirement and dependency lists, so they are tracked as signals like the Contact Clause. Costs the tree its only representation of political common cause.
2. **Move the child-born requirement from Tier 2 to Tier 3's definition of a settled population, or drop it from the tier lists and keep it as a tracked node.** Confidence: moderate. Standard practice: a judgment call about what the word settlement means; the definition in definitions.md is John's. Alternative: keep it and accept that the headline is really "the probability that the Belt is a place families live", which is a stricter and arguably truer reading of the show.
3. **Add a correlation mechanism to the compute script, or a stated adjustment in the methodology.** The simplest honest version: a per-run "world draw" that shifts every node's probability up or down together by a stated amount, so the scenarios report a range rather than a product of independent draws. Confidence: moderate; it is the standard fix for correlated conjunctions, but it adds one parameter that has to be argued for. Alternative: leave independence in, say so loudly in methodology.md, and let the numbers be a floor. Cheapest; makes the headline hard to defend against the first reader who notices.
4. **Tier 1's requirement list is US-specific.** Tier 1 says an outpost by anyone; the requirements name the US program. Confidence: high. Fix rolls into item 1.

None of these change a probability. All of them change the structure, which is what step 10 says to do.

## Rulings and the second run, 2026-09-19

John ruled on all four proposals the same evening: reword the two regime gates to durable, nation-agnostic form (which also settles the US-specific Tier 1 list); move the child-born-off-Earth requirement from Tier 2 to Tier 4; add the world draw to the compute script. Changes made:

- `R-crewed-lunar-program-outlives-two-changes-of-president`: any nation's or company's crewed lunar program counts, a cancelled program can be restarted, and a later program can reach the mark. Re-estimated 0.75 / 0.80 / 0.85 / 0.90 / 0.92 (was 0.60 flat). Id kept: the number it carried was hours old and never published; the revision log on the node records the change.
- `R-space-funding-holds-through-a-recession`: the test is now recovery to within ten percent within three years after a recession, tested at every recession, and a fail leaves the node open. Re-estimated 0.80 / 0.82 / 0.85 / 0.90 / 0.90 (was 0.55 flat). Id kept for the same reason.
- `data/tiers.toml`: `B-human-child-born-off-earth` moved from Tier 2's requirements to Tier 4's. The node is unchanged.
- `scripts/compute.py`: each play-through draws one number for how favourable the world is (normal, mean zero, spread 1.0 on the log-odds scale, set by `--world-spread`) and shifts every open world node's probability by it, so shared causes move nodes together. One standard deviation up turns 0.50 into 0.73 and 0.20 into 0.40. `run` prints the independent-roll result beside it so the gap stays visible. The spread is the one parameter the methodology (step 12) has to argue for.

The two re-estimates were proposed by Claude with the rewording and accepted by John in the same ruling.
