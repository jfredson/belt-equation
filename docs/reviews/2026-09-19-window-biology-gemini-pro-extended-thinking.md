**FDA accepts a biological aging test to replace waiting for patients to die**
Waiting for patients to develop diseases or die in lifespan trials takes years or decades, which is the primary bottleneck for aging drug development. If regulators accept a validated biomarker that proves a drug works in months, the timeline for the first approved drug collapses dramatically.

**Emergency surgery succeeds off Earth**
You cannot establish a settlement of thousands of people (Tier 2) without the ability to manage acute, non-evacuable medical emergencies like appendicitis or severe trauma. Off-Earth surgery introduces massive unknown variables regarding anesthesia metabolism, bleeding in partial gravity, and fluid management that must be solved before large populations arrive.

**Staple food crop harvested off Earth**
Current space agriculture focuses on leafy greens for morale and supplemental vitamins. Providing more than half of a crew's calories requires staple crops (carbohydrates and proteins) which demand vastly different scale, soil mechanics, and harvesting infrastructure. This is a hard prerequisite before a closed-loop food system can happen.

**B-closed-loop-life-support-year-off-earth**
A 50% chance by 2071 is far too high for growing more than half of a crew's calories off Earth. While recycling water and oxygen is largely solved today, farming requires massive pressurized greenhouses, lighting, and harvesting equipment. Shipping dehydrated food is incredibly mass-efficient. Until an off-Earth population scales into the thousands, the economic and logistical incentives heavily favor launching food on rockets rather than building complex off-world farms. The timeline for this node should push much further into the future.

**W-human-therapy-cuts-death-rate-in-older-adults**
The original 0.70 probability for the "no deadline" scenario vastly underestimates the long-term trajectory of medical science. A 25% reduction in all-cause mortality for the over-65 population roughly equates to functionally eliminating a major killer like cancer or heart disease. Given an infinite timeframe ("no deadline"), assuming our technological civilization endures, it is near-certain that medical advancements will achieve this either through curing major age-related diseases or addressing fundamental aging mechanisms.

**W-dog-rapamycin-trial-reports-longer-life**
This node specifically wagers on the success of the Dog Aging Project's TRIAD trial. Long-term veterinary trials are highly susceptible to administrative and financial failure before they conclude. The Dog Aging Project has historically faced severe NIH funding crises. While the scientific chance of rapamycin extending lifespan may be near 45%, the operational chance of *this specific trial* securing consistent funding and successfully crossing the finish line without collapsing is significantly lower.
