Started 2026-09-08. This document records what the project's terms mean and the decisions behind them. Each section is marked either DECIDED (with the date) or OPEN (with the current proposal). Nothing here gets a probability yet; probabilities come once the tree is stable.

Alternatives considered: none yet.

## What "Belter" means (the access tiers)

OPEN. A Belter is someone who lives and works off Earth as a matter of ordinary life, the way the Belters of The Expanse do. Because that can be true to different degrees, the project uses tiers. Proposed:

- **A0, contributes from Earth.** Works in or for the off-world industry without leaving: training crews, working for a launch or habitat company, writing about it.
- **A1, has flown.** At least one trip to space, suborbital or orbital, as passenger or crew.
- **A2, works off Earth on rotation.** Spends months at a time living and working at an off-world site, the way people work Antarctica or an oil platform today.
- **A3, lives off Earth.** Permanent residence at an off-world site.

Decision needed: which tier the headline number refers to. Proposal: A2, since working out there on rotation is what a Belter actually is; A3 is reported as a stretch. Belter-flavored names for the tiers are welcome.

## What "the Belt exists" means (the system tiers)

OPEN. The state of the solar system is also tiered, because the access tiers depend on it. Proposed:

- **Tier 1, Outpost.** Permanent crewed presence on the Moon and Mars, hundreds of people, fully supplied from Earth.
- **Tier 2, Settlement.** Thousands living off Earth, at least one child born and raised there, partial local self-sufficiency in air, water, and food.
- **Tier 3, Slow Expanse.** Tens of thousands across the Moon, Mars, and at least one asteroid-belt or outer-moon site. Transit times in months. A resource economy where something mined off Earth is sold at a profit. Nuclear propulsion in routine use.
- **Tier 4, Full Expanse.** Millions off Earth, transit times in weeks on constant-acceleration drives, a Belt population with its own political identity and economy.

Decision needed: whether Tier 3 counts as "the Belt exists" for the headline number. Proposal: yes; the A2 number depends mostly on Tier 3.

## The factors

OPEN. Eight factors, each a roll-up of a subtree of nodes: W window, L launch, E energy, D drive, B biology, M motive, R regime, A access. See README.md for one-line meanings.

Decision needed: keep E (energy) and D (drive) separate, or merge them. Keeping them separate is a bet that cheap power and fast propulsion are separable breakthroughs. Proposal: keep separate, and record the dependency (most D nodes depend on E nodes) in the tree.

## The window

OPEN. The deadline is John's expected date of natural death. It is a node in the tree, not a constant, because longevity research moves it.

Proposed for tonight: a provisional placeholder from standard life tables for a fit 40-year-old male born January 1986, marked provisional, to be refined from his health record in a later session. Proposed longevity scenarios to re-run the tree against: baseline (no extension beyond current trends), moderate extension, strong extension.

Decision needed: the placeholder date, and whether to publish two or three scenarios.

## The personal pathway (the A branch)

OPEN. Facts only John can supply that seed the access nodes: expected length of military service, what he wants to do afterward, willingness to relocate for a spaceflight-industry role, credentials being pursued. Health nodes stay out of the public tree and live in the Health folder.

Candidate nodes already identified: a civilian survival and contingency training industry for commercial crews exists; someone has made the move from military survival instruction into spaceflight training; analog missions accept applicants with his profile; a commercial orbital seat costs less than some threshold; eligibility for private orbital flight holds at 55, 60, and 65; post-service degree and credential completion.
