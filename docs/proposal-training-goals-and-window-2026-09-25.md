## What the comparison showed

The training goals on file today are all peak-performance goals with a hard date: score 100 on the SERE physical fitness test by 2026-12-31 (16 pull-ups, 75 sit-ups, 85 push-ups, 2 miles under 12:00), run 2 miles under 11:20 by 2026-12-31, graduate the pipeline by 2027-06-30. They serve one node, A-pipeline-graduation (0.85), which is the gate on the whole access branch and the only node in the tree John personally moves before May 2027. That aim is right and nothing below touches the current Runna block or the Jan 4 2027 peak.

Three gaps follow from the tree.

1. The gate node's largest personal risk is injury, not fitness. The Airborne medical hold in June 2026 is the precedent. No goal on file names it.
2. The window factor is priced from "the factors with the best evidence of moving all-cause mortality": aerobic capacity, strength and lean mass, not race speed. Astra's 2026-09-25 individual forecast also separates being alive from being flight-fit (roughly 77 percent flight-fit given alive at 85), and flight fitness is cardiovascular, bone and musculoskeletal function. No goal on file is a capacity goal with no end date.
3. definitions.md permits exactly one training-derived fact into the public tree: "a fitness percentile ... only if it comes from a repeatable measured test against a named age-group standard, stated with the standard and the date." Nothing John currently does on the track produces that number.

The biology factor adds a fourth, softer point: every B node stresses bone, muscle and cardiovascular deconditioning in partial gravity, and the countermeasures flown today are resistance loading and aerobic work. Rucking and the strength and power days already are the terrestrial version. This is a reason to keep loaded work in the lifelong template, not a new goal.

## Decision 1: three goal horizons in TimeAssembler

Keep the existing goals and add two horizons beside them, so the goal list reads as the tree does.

Gate horizon (to 2027-05, the pipeline). Existing goals unchanged, plus:
- "Arrive at INDOC on 2027-01-04 and at every pipeline phase uninjured: zero medical holds, zero training weeks lost to injury." Project: SERE Pipeline. Timeframe: year, target 2027-05-31.

Fork horizon (to 2031, the six-year mark, which is also when the first window leaves report: the rapamycin dog trial and the metformin composite trial). One goal:
- "Hold instructor-grade fitness through the first contract: pass every annual PT test at the top band and keep the annual age-graded number (Decision 2) at or above its 2027 baseline." Project: Air Force Career. Timeframe: year, renewed each January.

Window horizon (no end date). One goal:
- "Preserve the capacities the window is priced on: aerobic capacity, strength, lean mass and bone density, measured once a year against a named age-group standard, with the number and standard entered in the window paragraph at the annual review." Project: Longevity. Timeframe: year, renewed each January.

## Decision 2: the one age-graded test

Two candidate standards, both repeatable, both named.

Option A, World Masters Athletics age grading from a counted-track 5K. John already runs 5K time trials on a 400 m track with counted laps and the H10 strap as source of record. The WMA tables give an age-graded percent for a 40-year-old man at 5000 m. His 2026-09-15 time of about 19:05 grades to roughly 68 percent (regional class; 70 is the next band, 80 is national class). Cost: one time trial a year he is running anyway. Weakness: definitions.md says "percentile" and an age grade is a percent of the world standard, not a population percentile, so the paragraph wording would need a one-word amendment.

Option B, a laboratory VO2max test scored against the Cooper Institute age and sex norms (the standard behind the ACSM percentile tables). A true percentile, and the strongest single predictor of all-cause mortality in the literature the window method leans on. Cost: a lab visit (the off-base testing that has been waiting on a car). Weakness: not free, and not repeatable on a whim.

Recommendation: adopt both, with A as the annual anchor and B whenever a lab test happens (target once every two years). The paragraph would then read, for example, "age-graded 68 percent at 5000 m (WMA 2023 tables, 2026-09-15); VO2max Nth percentile for age (Cooper Institute norms, date)." A strength measure (grip strength against published age norms) is a cheap third line if wanted, but the two above are enough for the paragraph.

## Decision 3: injury as a written goal with a definition

The goal in Decision 1 needs a test so it can be scored. Proposed: a week counts as lost if the prescribed run plan or duty PT is replaced by rest or modified activity on medical direction for three or more days of that week. Score at each weekly review; the Jan 4 2027 test and the pipeline are the two resolution points.

## Decision 4: cadence tied to the annual review

- The age-graded 5K is run in the week before the first Sunday of January (first: the week of 2026-12-28, inside exodus, which is off Air Force programming and already the week the run plan owns). If it conflicts with the Jan 4 2027 peak, it moves to the week after the PT test in 2027 only; every later year it sits in the pre-review week.
- The number enters the window paragraph in definitions.md at the annual review (first: 2027-01-03), with the standard and the date, under the existing rule that nothing else from the health record crosses.
- The three horizon goals are re-read at the same review, beside the headline number, so training goals and the window are always revised on the same day from the same page.

## Decision 5: the access-branch training links stay a sketch

The tree rules the personal access branch provisional until pipeline graduation. Training goals that depend on it (medical eligibility for a rotation role, flight-fitness screening items) are not written now. Items for the May 2027 access-branch review, recorded so they are not lost: a bone-density and lean-mass baseline (DEXA) as the private analogue of the B factor's partial-gravity nodes; which of the private health metrics, if any, ever earn a public node; whether the fork choice should be run with a training-load parameter (the military route keeps the duty PT structure, the civilian route does not).

## What stays private

All raw numbers (times, VO2max, DEXA, labs) stay in ~/Documents/Workspace/Health and TimeAssembler Longevity. Only the age-graded percent or percentile, the named standard and the date cross into definitions.md. This is the rule already in force; the proposal only gives it something to carry.
