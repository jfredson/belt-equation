- Ledger step 29 done, what a step is worth and what an entry was worth: `compute.py worth` holds every open step the headline rests on at yes, plays the whole tree out again, then holds it at no and plays it out again — that difference is the step's worth, and it is what the site's watch lists and worth lines are built from, so they can never drift from the tree. A step the headline does not rest on at any remove is recorded as worth nothing without being played out, which is exact and halves the work; twenty-six of the fifty-five open steps are in that position, most of them feeding tiers above the one the headline asks about. `compute.py attribute` asks the question backwards: it undoes one ledger entry's changes and only those, plays the tree out again, and reports how far the headline falls; `--write` commits the answer to `data/attribution/`, which the site reads rather than recomputing history on every build. Contributions do not add up to a run's whole move and the leftover is shown as its own line, because changes in one run interact and some — a requirement moved between tiers, a change to how the tree is played out — cannot be undone one at a time at all. Holding a step at an outcome leaves the roll of the dice in place, so a run with nothing forced draws exactly the numbers it always did: every published figure is unchanged.
